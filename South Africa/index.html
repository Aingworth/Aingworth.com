The Gap

South African businesses adopted AI fast. Most stopped at the shallow end.

Walk into most firms in most towns and someone is already using ChatGPT. To write the odd email. To rephrase a paragraph. Used like that, AI is a slightly faster way to do the same day you had before.

The businesses pulling ahead are using it differently: structured workflows for quoting, follow-ups, reporting, marketing and admin, built once and reused every day. That frees the owner and the team to do the work that actually earns money. The difference between the two is not budget or talent. It is a few hours of practical training built around your kind of business.

That is what this programme does. No hype, no jargon, no theory about robots. Just the systems, applied.

Common Questions

Questions sponsors and owners ask.

Is the training available in Afrikaans?
Yes. Every keynote and workshop is delivered in English or Afrikaans, whichever your room prefers. The content, frameworks and playbook are identical in both languages. Lucy is fully bilingual.
What does it cost?
Local programme rates: R15,000 for a keynote, R20,000 to R25,000 for a half-day working session, R35,000 for a full day, plus travel outside Gauteng. Public per-seat sessions run from R950 per person with a minimum of 15 seats. Corporate engagements are priced separately on the services page.
Who pays for the training?
It depends how the session comes together. Often someone brings a room of local businesses together and covers the session, so everyone attending comes free. Other times people simply book a seat for themselves. Either way works, and if you are not sure which applies to you, just ask.
My business is just me. Is this still worth it?
Especially then. When you are the whole business, every hour you spend on admin is an hour not spent earning. One-person businesses usually see the fastest return from this, because there is nobody to delegate to and the time comes straight back to you.
Who is this training for?
No. It is for anyone running a business in South Africa, or starting one: a shop, a practice, a farm, a workshop, a service business, a side business becoming something bigger. One employee or a hundred. Sessions are adapted to whoever is in the room.
What is in the playbook?
A written guide to everything covered in the session: the prompts, the workflows, the frameworks applied to your type of business, and a set of first projects to run in your own firm. It is written so a team member who missed the session can still follow it.
What happens after the training?
Every workshop includes 30 days of email and WhatsApp support. After that, businesses that want ongoing help can book one-to-one coaching or a light monthly retainer. The playbook stands on its own either way.
Where do you travel?
Anywhere in South Africa. This programme was built for the smaller towns and cities, not only the metros. Travel outside Gauteng is billed at cost.
