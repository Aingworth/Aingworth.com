Common Questions

Questions business owners ask.

Is the AI training available in Afrikaans?
Yes. Every session is delivered in English or Afrikaans, whichever you prefer. The content, workflows and playbook are identical in both languages. Lucy Aingworth is fully bilingual and also gives talks and media interviews in Afrikaans.
What does the program cost?
From R8,500 per month with a minimum three-month commitment. That includes one private one-to-one session per month with you as the business owner (online or in person), personalized AI workflows built around your business, a prompt library, a custom AI Playbook, and email and WhatsApp support between sessions. Travel outside Gauteng is billed at cost. Corporate keynotes and workshops are a separate engagement, priced on the services page.
Why is there a three-month minimum?
Because a once-off workshop does not change how a business runs. In month one we build your first working workflows. In month two you apply them and we fix what breaks. In month three they become the way your business operates. Anything shorter is a presentation, not implementation.
My business is just me. Is this still worth it?
Especially then. When you are the whole business, every hour you spend on admin is an hour not spent earning. One-person businesses usually see the fastest return, because there is nobody to delegate to and the time comes straight back to you.
Do I need to be technical?
No. There is no coding, no jargon and no theory about robots. If you can type a WhatsApp message, you can do everything in this program. Everything is demonstrated using real examples from your own business.
Which AI tools does the program cover?
ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Kimi and other Large Language Models: what each one is good at, when to use which, and how to build repeatable workflows with them. The principles are tool-agnostic, so what you learn survives the next round of new releases.
What is the AI Playbook?
A custom written guide to your business's AI systems that grows with every session: the prompts, the workflows, the SOPs, applied to your business specifically. It is written so a team member who missed a session can still follow it, and it stays yours after the program ends.
Are sessions in person or online?
Either. Sessions run online or in person anywhere in South Africa, whichever works for your business. Travel outside Gauteng is billed at cost.
What happens after three months?
You choose. Many businesses continue month to month as new processes get systemized. Others stop, and everything built during the program, the workflows, the prompt library and the playbook, stays theirs and keeps working. No lock-in either way.
Can my whole team use this?
The monthly sessions are one-to-one with you as the business owner, so the training stays focused and moves at your pace. But everything built is designed to roll out to your team: the prompt library and the AI Playbook are written so anyone on staff can follow and use them. If you'd rather have your team trained directly in the sessions, that's a separate team engagement, priced on scope — just ask and we'll quote it.
