The Frameworks

The vocabulary of recognition.

Every idea in Become the Answer has a name, because naming a problem is the first step to solving it. These are the frameworks behind every audit, strategy, and engagement at Aingworth. Each one is available as a downloadable PDF.

Anyone can give recommendations. The difference is a way of thinking that consistently produces better decisions.

These frameworks were developed through a decade of SEO, CRO, and digital strategy work across global markets, and sharpened into the system published in Become the Answer. They exist to do one thing: turn the vague anxiety of "AI is changing search" into a set of named, diagnosable, fixable problems.

Together they describe the world you now operate in, the systems that judge you, the conditions you have to meet, and the process for meeting them.

From the Book

Seven named ideas. One system.

Each framework is a chapter of the same argument. Download any of them as a one-page PDF reference.

01

The Core Framework

The Recognition Triangle

The three conditions AI systems use to decide who to recognise, cite, and recommend.

Clarity is what you stand for. Credibility is what can be verified. Earned Authority is what others say when you are not in the room. Recognition forms only where all three hold, and decays the moment one weakens.

02

The Process

The Authority Method

The five-stage process for building authority that machines and people both trust.

Diagnose where recognition is leaking. Define what you stand for without ambiguity. Build the verifiable signals. Distribute them where the systems look. Refine as the landscape shifts. It is a cycle, not a checklist.

Download PDF
03

The New Reality

The Machine Gatekeeper

The AI systems that now sit between you and your audience, deciding who gets cited.

Your next client does not scan ten links anymore. They ask a system, and the system answers. That system is the new gatekeeper: it decides who is mentioned, who is recommended, and who is invisible. You cannot charm it. You can only be legible to it.

Download PDF
04

The Hidden Cost

Authority Debt

The accumulated cost of years spent chasing visibility metrics instead of building verifiable authority.

Every quarter spent optimising for clicks instead of citations compounds quietly. When the system changed, the debt came due: brands with strong traffic and weak authority found themselves invisible in AI answers. The debt is repayable, but only deliberately.

Download PDF
05

The Shift

The Recognition Economy

The move from competing to be seen to competing to be retrieved.

The attention economy rewarded whoever shouted loudest. The recognition economy rewards whoever the systems can define, verify, and cite. Attention was rented. Recognition is owned, and it compounds.

Download PDF
06

The Risk

Brand Hallucination

When an AI system confidently describes your brand inaccurately, because you gave it nothing better.

AI systems do not say "I don't know." They fill gaps with plausible guesses. If your signals are thin, inconsistent, or contradictory, the system invents the rest, and prospects meet the invention before they ever meet you.

Download PDF
07

The Mechanism

The Composite Picture

The aggregate impression AI systems assemble about you from every signal on the web.

No single page decides how a system sees you. It assembles a composite from everything: your site, your profiles, what others publish, what the data says. The work is making every fragment tell the same story, so the composite is one you would recognise.
