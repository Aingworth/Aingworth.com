A Name, Not A New Offer

Why this is now called Recognition Snapshot.

If you were quoted, referred, or previously spoke with us about the AI Visibility Audit, you are in the right place. Recognition Snapshot is that same engagement. Aingworth's whole vocabulary is built around recognition, not visibility: the Recognition Triangle, the Recognition Diagnostic, Recognition Management. Visibility was the old game, being seen and ranked. Recognition is the new one, being named and trusted. Carrying an offer under the old "visibility" language while everything else on this site speaks in "recognition" terms was a naming gap, not a strategy. Recognition Snapshot closes that gap.

What has not changed

The price. The scope. The five-to-ten-business-day turnaround. The person doing the work. Only the name is different.

What The Snapshot Covers

The same three conditions, every time.

Every Recognition Snapshot checks your brand against the Recognition Triangle, the model at the center of Become the Answer. A brand becomes the answer AI gives only when all three conditions hold together.

01

Clarity

What you actually stand for.

The unambiguous definition of who you are, what you do, and who it is for. If a machine cannot define you, it cannot retrieve you.

02

Credibility

What can be verified.

The proof, track record, and validation that let people and systems trust the claim you make about yourself.

03

Earned Authority

What others say when you are not in the room.

The citations and references that cannot be bought or faked. The condition most brands are missing, and the one AI weighs most.
