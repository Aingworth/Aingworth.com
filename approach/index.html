Our Approach

The thesis, and the method that proves it.

Become the Answer sets out a belief: that AI-driven discovery rewards recognition, not visibility. This page is that belief translated into a model you can check and a method you can run, the same one behind every audit, strategy, and engagement at Aingworth.

In one paragraph

Aingworth's approach rests on two ideas from Become the Answer: How Brands Build Authority in the Age of AI. The Recognition Triangle defines the three conditions a brand needs to be recognized, cited, and recommended by AI: Clarity, Credibility, and Earned Authority. The Authority Method is the five-stage process Aingworth uses to build them: Diagnose, Define, Build, Distribute, Refine. Together, they turn brand authority from a hunch into a measured, repeatable system.

Scattered signals. One recognized answer.

The Thesis

Visibility is being seen. Recognition is being chosen.

The search box is going quiet. People increasingly ask a machine a question and get one answer, not ten blue links. The brands in that answer did not get there by being the loudest. They got there by being the ones a machine could define, verify, and trust enough to name.

We do not treat this as a problem to panic about. It is a system to understand, and it rewards a specific kind of brand: one with a clear claim, evidence behind the claim, and other people saying it when the brand is not in the room. That combination is what we call recognition, and building it deliberately is the whole of our approach.

The Old Game

Visibility

  • Be seen, rank, get the click
  • Measured in traffic and impressions
  • Rented: stops the moment spend stops
  • Can be bought with ad spend

The New Game

Recognition

  • Be the answer, not a link on a list
  • Measured in citation and recommendation
  • Owned: compounds the longer it is held
  • Cannot be bought, only earned
The Model

Three conditions held at once.

A brand is not simply visible or invisible to AI. It is recognized, named, described, and recommended, only when three conditions hold together. Miss one, and recognition decays.

01 · Clarity

What you actually stand for.

The unambiguous definition of who you are, what you do, and who it is for. If a machine cannot define you, it cannot retrieve you. No clean definition, no recognition.

02 · Credibility

What can be verified.

The proof, track record, and validation that let people and systems trust the claim you make about yourself. Credibility turns a clear claim into a believable one.

03 · Earned Authority

What others say when you are not in the room.

Citations, references, and recognition that cannot be bought and cannot be faked. This is the condition most brands are missing, and the hardest one to build.

Common Questions

Questions people ask about our approach.

What is Aingworth's approach?
Aingworth's approach combines two ideas from Become the Answer: the Recognition Triangle, which defines the three conditions of recognition (Clarity, Credibility, Earned Authority), and the Authority Method, the five-stage process (Diagnose, Define, Build, Distribute, Refine) used to build them.
Does Aingworth guarantee AI visibility or citations?
No. Aingworth builds and measures the conditions that increase the probability of accurate recognition, citation, and recommendation across AI-driven discovery, and reports honestly on what changes. A guarantee of specific AI behavior would not be an honest claim, so we do not make one.
How is this different from SEO or GEO?
SEO and GEO tactics are inputs to recognition, not the goal itself. A page can rank first in search and still appear in none of the AI answers a buyer reads. The Authority Method targets the second game: being the answer itself, not just a ranked link.
Where can I read the full definitions and frameworks?
Every framework named on this page, and the others from Become the Answer, has its own definition page at /frameworks/, each with a downloadable one-page PDF.
