Authority Signal

A piece of evidence that establishes a brand's expertise or standing.

Aingworth Concept

Full explanation

An authority signal is a piece of evidence, first-party, third-party, editorial, or institutional, that helps establish a brand's expertise, credibility, or standing within its category.

Why this matters

Signals are what a system actually works with. Expertise that has never been documented, referenced, or published produces no signal at all, however real it is.

Where this sits

Authority Signal belongs to Recognition & Authority, one of the seven layers of the Aingworth Glossary. What Aingworth believes. Recognition is the outcome; authority is the evidence that makes it possible. These are the terms that belong to Aingworth, most of them first set out in Become the Answer.

Related terms

Recognition Signal Information that helps a system correctly identify and recall a brand. Trust Signals The cues that help people and systems judge reliability. Earned Authority What others say about a brand when it is not in the room.
