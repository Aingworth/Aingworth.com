        How it works

        One screenshot proves nothing. A pattern does.

        01

        Ask a real buyer question

        Not your brand name. The question a customer actually types when they are deciding who to trust, like "who are the best consultants for this."

        02

        Run it several times

        The same question goes to the model five or more times. AI answers are probabilistic, so one answer is an anecdote. Repeated runs are evidence.

        03

        Read the pattern

        You get a Recognition Rate, plus the messages AI always says, sometimes says, and rarely says about your category. That is what the machine believes.

        What it is for

        A recognition instrument, not a vanity score.

        Track how consistently AI names you for the questions that lead to real work, and watch the number move as authority is built.

        Competitive intelligence

        See who the models reach for first in your category, and where the recognition is still up for grabs.

        Content and positioning
